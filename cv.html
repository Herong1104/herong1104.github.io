Herong Bai

Pianist · Ningxia, China

Piano Studies

  • Current teacher: Cliburn Artist Fei-Fei Dong.

  • Studies and masterclasses: Alexander Kobrin, Boris Berman, Robert Shannon, Jihye Chang, Alvin Chow, Haewon Song, Marina Lomazov, Ernest Barretta, Angela Cheng, Leslie Howard, among others.
