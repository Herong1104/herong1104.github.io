Research

Aviation Research

Data analysis & wind-shear detection.

2023–2025

Flight Data & Recovery

A research project examining operational flight data and nonlinear patterns in the recovery of air traffic after COVID-related policy changes.

My contribution

Herong cleaned and integrated datasets containing more than 300,000 flight records, built time-series visualisations, and analysed changes in traffic patterns over time.

2024–2025

LiDAR Wind-Shear Detection

The research team developed an integrated wind-shear monitoring and early-warning system, bringing together YOLO-based image recognition, Transformer-based 3D LiDAR point-cloud detection, and data from multiple sources.

Dataset preparation

Under the guidance of graduate researchers and research staff, Herong learned and contributed to dataset preparation and model evaluation. She used Python to write scripts for denoising, registration and segmentation of raw LiDAR point clouds, helping prepare standardised datasets for training and evaluation.

Evaluation & visualisation

She wrote automated evaluation scripts and contributed to comparative and ablation experiments, using metrics such as mean average precision (mAP) to help assess the effects of model optimisation. In Origin, she prepared figures from intermediate results and final test outputs, with consistent layouts, axes and colour schemes for project reports.

Project reference

Wang Bo, Wang Haixia & Zhang Zhicheng. A Point-Cloud-Based Wind-Shear Recognition System. Internet of Things Technologies, 2026, no. 1, pp. 21–25.
