Community projects ↗

Music for Every Child

One shared piano, a room of young learners, and music taught together. At Tongxinyuan Community, Herong provides free piano lessons through her work with the Ningxia Library.

In 2026, she arranged 44 concert tickets so that her students and their families could hear Grieg’s Piano Concerto live.
