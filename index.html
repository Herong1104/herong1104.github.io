HERONG BAI

Short Bio

Piano, studies & community

Herong Bai is a pianist from Ningxia, northwestern China, who began studying piano at the age of four and currently studies with Cliburn Artist Fei-Fei Dong.

Alongside her work at the piano, Herong develops a connected approach to learning mathematics, organizing concepts across grade levels so that new understanding can grow from what came before.

MATHEMATICS & EDUCATION

Rebuilding Mathematical Learning

Learn Less, Learn Better

I organize mathematics around the principles connecting its parts, so that later ideas extend earlier understanding. Over six years, a single sheet of notes grew into more than 800 pages of explanations, diagrams and derivations. My independent manuscript develops this approach to teaching and learning across Grades 1–12.
