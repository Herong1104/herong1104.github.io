Research & Inquiry

I have spent much of my childhood trying to understand why we are taught to learn the same ideas over and over again, each time under a different name. What began as a way to save time for piano practice gradually became a habit of rebuilding entire subjects around the principles connecting them. I first did this with mathematics, then carried the same questions into economics, research, and public policy. I am interested in what happens when we stop treating formulas, markets, institutions, and people's lives as separate problems and begin examining the relationships among them.

Aviation Research

Flight Data, AI & Wind-Shear Detection | 2023–2025

My aviation research has ranged from analyzing more than 300,000 operational flight records to preparing and evaluating three-dimensional LiDAR datasets for an AI-based wind-shear monitoring project. Working alongside researchers and technical specialists, I contributed Python-based data processing, automated evaluation, comparative experiments, and scientific visualization.
